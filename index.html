Research

Questions that move between evidence and practice.

My work often begins with applied problems: how people sleep, seek help, recover after collective stress, communicate across political difference, and build meaningful routines. The methods range from randomized trials and longitudinal designs to text mining, machine learning, diary studies, and mixed methods.

Mental Health and Well-being

Online counselling, text-based mental health services, sleep interventions, boredom, smartphone use, solitude, and everyday routines.

Digital Life and Attention

How attention, meaning, boredom beliefs, and digital habits shape behavior for young people, working adults, and online communities.

Political and Family Psychology

Political incongruence, moral foundations, social unrest, collective action, dehumanization, and communication in families.

Nature, Disaster, and Community Resilience

Nature exposure, post-disaster mental health, resource-scarce intervention models, and collective resources for thriving communities.
